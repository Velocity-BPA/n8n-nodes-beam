# Automate Beam Blockchain Operations with Our New n8n Community Node

We're excited to announce the release of n8n-nodes-beam, a powerful new community node that brings Beam blockchain automation directly into your n8n workflows!

## The Challenge

Blockchain developers and businesses working with Beam have long struggled with manual processes for wallet management, NFT operations, and DeFi interactions. Whether you're managing multiple wallets, processing NFT transactions for gaming applications, or integrating with the Sphere marketplace, these repetitive tasks consume valuable time and resources that could be better spent on innovation.

## Introducing n8n-nodes-beam

Velocity BPA is proud to present n8n-nodes-beam, a comprehensive solution that bridges the gap between Beam blockchain capabilities and n8n's powerful automation platform. This node empowers you to automate complex blockchain workflows without writing extensive code.

## Key Features

**Wallet Management**: Seamlessly manage Beam wallets within your workflows, enabling automated balance checks, transaction monitoring, and fund transfers.

**NFT & Gaming Operations**: Streamline NFT minting, transfers, and metadata management - perfect for gaming studios and digital collectible platforms.

**Sphere Marketplace Integration**: Connect directly with Beam's Sphere marketplace to automate listing monitoring, purchase notifications, and inventory management.

**DeFi Automation**: Build sophisticated DeFi workflows including liquidity management, yield tracking, and automated trading strategies.

## Getting Started

Installing n8n-nodes-beam is straightforward. Simply run:


npm install n8n-nodes-beam


After installation, restart your n8n instance, and you'll find the Beam node available in your node palette, ready to integrate into your workflows.
